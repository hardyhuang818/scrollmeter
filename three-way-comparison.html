Drag the CPU load slider: at 0% all three look similar; at 30% the rightmost starts dropping frames; at 60%+ the rightmost is nearly stuck, middle stutters occasionally, leftmost stays rock solid. This is the architectural reason Safari > Chrome > Electron on macOS.
